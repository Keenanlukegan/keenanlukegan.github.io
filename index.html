What we automate

Three kinds of work leave your week.

Each one is built around your business, on the tools you already use.

  • Back-Office Automation

    The admin your team does by hand every month, automated and run for you.

    • Invoicing: bills raised and tracked each month.
    • Scheduling: timetables, rooms and staff in one view.
    • Staff pay: hours and sessions added up for payday.
    • Month-end: the month's figures pulled together from your records.
    • Records: one place for every customer's details.

  • Customer-Facing Automation

    The messages and bookings your customers wait on, sent and handled automatically.

    • Reminders: classes and appointments, sent ahead of time.
    • Attendance: marked once and kept on record.
    • Booking and make-ups: slots offered and booked without a phone call.
    • Progress reports: updates sent to each customer.
    • Bills and receipts: sent to the right person each time.

  • Company Brain

    Your business's records and know-how in one place, for you and your team to ask and work from.

    • Customer and staff records, connected.
    • How you do things: prices, policies and procedures, written down once.
    • Questions answered from your own records.
    • Built to order, around your business.

    We put AI on the last mile of the boring tasks, and nowhere near the part that's yours.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What does KEEN Intelligent Systems do?

KEEN automates the manual, repetitive admin a small business still does by hand, on the tools it already uses, and runs it every month. That covers back-office work like invoicing, scheduling, staff pay and month-end, and customer-facing work like reminders, attendance, bookings and progress reports.

What is the free Fix?

Within a week of getting your records, we redo last month of one monthly admin task you name, free, so you can judge on your own numbers whether you want it run this way every month. It starts once your records are in.

How much does it cost?

For a business with staff, the Fix is free. Then it's S$2,900 for the first 12 weeks, which covers the build and three months of us running it, and S$500 a month after that, with 30 days' notice. For a one-person practice, we build it free, it runs beside your records through one month-end, and then it's S$65 a month.

Do I have to change software or learn a new app?

No. We build on the tools you already use. At our own school we tried moving everyone to a new app. Teachers ended up typing the same thing in three places, so we went back to what they knew and built there.

What if it doesn't work?

If a month-end doesn't match your own invoices, we keep working on it at no charge until it does. It covers what our system controls.

Is this an AI product?

AI does the repeatable last step of a routine task. It does not do the creative or judgement part of your work, and a person stays on it.

How much of my time does it take?

About 20 minutes a week once it runs, to read what went out, what came in and what got fixed. During the build, there's one call a week and an update every day.

What happens if I stop?

Everything is built in your own accounts, so it stays yours. You give 30 days' notice.
